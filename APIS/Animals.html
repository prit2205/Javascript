Negros Fruit Dove

Ptilinopus arcanus

📍 Negros Island, Philippines

The Negros fruit dove (Ptilinopus arcanus) is a species of bird in the pigeon and dove family, Columbidae. It is endemic to the island of Negros in the Philippines and known from a single specimen collected on Mount Kanlaon in 1953. Habitat destruction likely forced the species to higher elevations.
